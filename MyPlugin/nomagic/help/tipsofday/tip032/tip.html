Quickly copying a diagram as an image to a clipboard

In the main menu, click Edit and choose one of the following:

  • Copy as BMP Image or press Ctrl + Shif + B.
  • Copy as EMF Image or press Ctrl + Shif + E.
  • Copy as JPG Image or press Ctrl + Shif + J.
  • Copy as PNG Image or press Ctrl + Shif + P.
